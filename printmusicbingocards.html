Printing Tips for the Best Results

A few things that make a real difference once you've got a PDF in hand:
  • Print two-up in landscape. Most of our game packs (and Bingo Card Generator Pro) support two cards per sheet — it roughly halves your paper and ink cost for a big group.
  • Use colored card stock. Plain white paper works fine, but a light cardstock in a color that matches your theme reads better across a room and holds up to a night of dabbers and drinks.
  • Cut carefully, or don't bother. Slicing two-up sheets in half gives a cleaner, more "official" look, but it's entirely optional — plenty of hosts just hand out the full sheet as-is.
  • Going virtual? Skip the printer. Every card downloads as a normal PDF, so remote guests can open it in Adobe Reader (or any PDF app that supports markup) and mark off squares right on their device.
  • Keep a callsheet. If you're building your own game rather than buying one of ours, jot your master list down in the order you plan to call it — you'll want it at the host table to settle any close calls.
