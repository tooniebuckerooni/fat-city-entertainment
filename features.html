The Green Room

Hosts talking shop

Fat City operator Aug 6, 2026

What do you get paid per game — and what's actually included?

Rates for this work are all over the map and nobody can find out what anything is worth without asking a stranger. So: what's your number, what's the format, how long is the game, and does the venue cover your tab?

We'll go first. These are Fat City's real numbers — Canadian dollars, weekday show, roughly three hours on site.

A one-off weekday show is $450. Almost nobody pays that, and it isn't a bluff price — a single night is genuinely the most expensive way to buy this. Nothing amortises. We price it to reflect that.

Booked as a recurring night it's $225. Three or four dates is the minimum that makes sense; twelve booked at once is what we're actually aiming for. There's one step past that: $200 flat if the venue puts a $25 tab behind the bar for the host. We'd rather give the tab than the $25, and further down this thread we'll explain why that trade is better for the venue than it looks.

Weekends, holidays and special events are full rate or higher. A two-host Christmas party starts at $900.

The part that catches new hosts out is the cost side. Budget about $50 a night for licensing games, printing cards and consumables. So on a $225 night you keep $175 for about three hours. On the $200-plus-tab deal you keep $150 and drink for free. Work out your costs before you agree to a number, not after.

That's ours. What's yours?
