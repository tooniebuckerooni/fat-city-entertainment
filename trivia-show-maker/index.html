Free Trivia Show Maker & Question Generator

The Trivia Show Maker is a free tool for building your own pub-trivia night and printing a complete, ready-to-host game kit. Write your own questions, drop in vetted sample packs, or let AI dig up categories you'd never think of — then download the host packet, question sheets, blank team answer sheets, and score sheet as print-ready PDFs. Everything runs in your browser, and once you print, the whole night runs on paper — no app, login, or wifi needed at the table.

How do I make trivia questions for a pub quiz night?

Add a round, then fill it three ways: type your own questions, drop in ready-made sample questions by category, or use the AI generator to write a whole round from any topic. Set each round's answer format (open-ended, true/false, or multiple choice), point value, age range, and difficulty, then print the matching packets.

Is the Trivia Show Maker free?

Yes. Building rounds, using the sample question packs, and printing your full game kit is free and entirely local to your browser. AI question and category generation is an optional paid add-on that runs on credits — the free product works fully without it.

Can I print the trivia game?

Yes. Every game exports as print-ready PDFs — a host packet with answers, a question packet for a reader, blank team answer sheets, and a score sheet. Once printed, the whole night runs on paper with no app or wifi at the table.

Can AI generate the categories and questions?

Yes. Dig for Categories suggests five surprising round ideas you can use or refine, and Generate 10 writes a full round of questions from any topic — matching the round's format, age range, and difficulty. The hook is inventive categories you'd struggle to come up with on your own.

Can I set the age range or difficulty?

Each round has an Age Range — Family, Kids, Teens, or Adults — that steers both the sample questions and the AI, plus an optional Difficulty (Balanced, Easy, Medium, or Hard) for AI generation, so a family fundraiser and a 21+ bar quiz each get the right questions.

Do players need an app or wifi?

No. The tool is only for building and printing the game beforehand. At the event, everything runs on the printed sheets — no screens, apps, or internet required for players or teams.
