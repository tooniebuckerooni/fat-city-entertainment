Two Bingo Card Generators. Pick the One That Fits.

We now run two card generators. The original still works exactly as it always has, and it isn't going anywhere. The newer one is more flexible if you need something beyond music bingo. Both are free to start.

The Original Generator

Built for music bingo, and still the fastest way to get a printable set of cards for a night you're running this week. Free, no account, runs right here on this site.

Best for: music bingo hosts who want cards now, with no setup.
Use the Original Generator

The All-Purpose Generator

Rebuilt from the ground up on its own site. Handles any kind of bingo game, not just music, with live card previews and custom styling. Nothing to install, and free to start.

Best for: custom or non-music bingo, or when you want more control over how the cards look.
Try the All-Purpose Generator

Which should I use?

If you're hosting music bingo and just need cards printed, the original does that in a couple of minutes and there's no reason to switch. If you're running a themed or non-music game, or you care about how the cards look, the All-Purpose Generator gives you more room to work.

We're keeping both available. If the newer one turns out to be better for everyone over time, we'll say so — but the original will stay up for the hosts who already rely on it.
