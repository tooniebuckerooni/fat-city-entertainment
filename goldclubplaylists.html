Playlist & Pack FAQ

What's the difference between the Starter Pack (Bronze), Silver Club, and Gold Club?

Same games, three sizes. Starter Pack (Bronze) is the 10 best-selling games by real sales data. Silver Club is the first 25 games in the collection. Gold Club is everything — all 50 games in one download. The packs themselves are all one-time purchases; each also bundles a free Bingo Card Generator 2.0 perk (Day Pass, 1 Month, or 1 Year) — only the Silver Club’s free month renews into a paid subscription if you don’t cancel.

Do I need Spotify or Apple Music to run these games?

No — the printable bingo cards work on their own. The Spotify and Apple Music links on this page are just a convenience: a ready-made playlist so you don't have to source and sequence 30-plus songs yourself.

Can I buy just one game instead of a bundle?

Yes. Every game on this page is also sold individually for $10.99 — click any game's name to go straight to its own page. Buying individually makes sense if you only need one specific theme; a bundle is cheaper per game every time.

Are the Starter Pack (Bronze), Silver Club, or Gold Club subscriptions?

The packs themselves are all one-time payments — download once, keep the files, host as many nights as you want. Two exceptions: Silver's bundled 1-Month-Free and Gold's bundled 1-Year-Free Bingo Card Generator 2.0 perks both continue as a paid Gen 2 subscription afterward unless you cancel. Only the Bronze Day Pass is a one-time bonus with no recurring charge.

How many bingo cards does each game print?

Up to 250 randomized cards per game, formatted to print cleanly on regular letter paper — reliable enough to run off at a real print shop (Staples, in our own testing), which is where a lot of other bingo-card tools start to fall apart.
