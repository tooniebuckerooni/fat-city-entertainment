A full period of English and Language Arts review that a class will actually play — vocabulary, grammar, the books a class has actually read, and the figures of speech they get tested on.

Five rounds, 50 questions and a tiebreaker. Run it as one lesson, or pull a single ten-question round as a Friday warm-up or an end-of-unit review. Everything is read aloud and answered on paper, so no projector, no devices and no textbook.

The rounds:

  • Words and Meanings
  • Grammar and Punctuation
  • Stories and Authors
  • Figures of Speech
  • True or False

What's included:

  • Teacher packet — every question in order, with the answers.
  • Team answer sheets — one per round, ready to photocopy.
  • Score sheet — room for up to 12 teams.
  • Tiebreaker — a closest-wins question for when two teams finish level.

The first two rounds are pitched easier and the later ones harder, so the class ramps rather than losing half the room in round one. Round four is worth double, which keeps a slow-starting team in it to the end.

Answers are written to be marked by a student swapping sheets with a neighbour: short, specific, and with the common near-miss noted where one should score.

Licensing. This price covers one teacher's own classroom — print and photocopy it for your students as often as you like. A school-wide licence, covering every teacher in the building, is $22.00 and is chosen at checkout.
