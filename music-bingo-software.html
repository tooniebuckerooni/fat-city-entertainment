Music Bingo Software for Hosts, DJs and Venues

Music bingo software turns a list of songs into a set of bingo cards where no two are the same, plus a call sheet for the host. That is the whole job. You bring a playlist and a speaker, the software makes the cards, and the room does the rest. We have run music bingo for live crowds since 1999, and everything here is built for a host who wants to print, press play and get on with the night.

What do you need music bingo software to do?

Three things. Shuffle the songs so every card is different, lay the cards out so they print cleanly, and give the host a list of what is on them. Some platforms go further and put the card on each player's phone, check winners for you and bill a monthly fee per venue. Ours does not do that. Our cards are printed or shared as a PDF, and a person calls bingo out loud. If you want phone play with automatic checking, a live-play platform is the better fit. If you want cards in your hand tonight with no subscription, read on.

Which tool fits your night?

Trying it for the first time: the free music bingo card generator on this site. Paste in at least 25 song titles, choose where the free space goes, and download a PDF. No sign-up.

Hosting regularly from your own song lists: Bingo Card Generator Pro is $74.50, once. Unlimited cards, no watermark, and no monthly fee.

Building from a playlist, in your own colours: our newer generator at BingoCardGenerator.Online reads a Spotify or Apple Music playlist export, lets you pick the grid size and colours, and makes cards you can print or share by link.

Do you need software at all?

Often you do not. If the hard part is choosing songs a mixed crowd will recognise, a ready-made game does that for you. Each of our music bingo games is a download of 250 randomized cards, a call sheet, and ready-made playlists on both Spotify and Apple Music. Need more than 250 cards, or the playlist on another music service? Ask us and we will send them. A single game such as The 90s is $11.99, and you can read every game's full song list free before you buy.

What else do you need to run a music bingo night?

A phone or laptop with a music app, a speaker the room can hear, printed cards, and something to mark them with. Plan on about 60 seconds of each song. Our guides cover the rest: the rules and ways to win, how many cards to print, and how to run a music bingo night from the first song to the last prize.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. The free generator is free, Generator Pro is a one-time purchase, and every game pack is yours once you download it. The newer generator has an optional pass or plan for hosts who make new cards every week.
