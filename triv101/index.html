Triv 101

Survey-style trivia meets a darts countdown. Every team starts at 101 and races down to exactly zero.

How to play: each turn, a team picks a number on the board. Read the question aloud and reveal the answers one at a time — the team can bank the points after any reveal, or push their luck for more. Get one wrong and the turn passes with nothing.

Help build the game. Take a quick survey — the answers everyone gives become the questions we all play with.
