# Email-Capture Gate + Welcome Email

Ready to implement on the new GitHub-hosted site. Currently ~109 downloads per period on the free generator's PDF walk away anonymous — this gate is the single fastest list-building lever available since the traffic already exists.

## Email-capture gate (in front of the PDF download)

**Placement:** Insert this as a lightweight step between "Generate my cards" and the actual PDF download/print action — not a full-page interstitial. Keep it to one field to minimize friction.

**Headline:** Your cards are ready 🎉 (drop the emoji if that doesn't match site tone)

**Body copy (one line):**
> Enter your email and we'll send your printable cards now — plus the occasional new playlist or hosting tip. No spam, unsubscribe anytime.

**Field:** Email address only (no name field — every extra field lowers completion).

**Button copy:** Get My Cards

**Microcopy under the button (trust signal):** We'll never sell your email. One email a week, max.

**Fallback / no-thanks path:** Keep a small "No thanks, just download" text link below the button rather than forcing the gate — a hard-blocked download will frustrate hosts who are mid-prep for tonight's event and generate support complaints. The goal is to capture the willing majority, not lose the free-tool goodwill that built this audience.

## Notes for implementation
- Trigger this email immediately (within 1-2 minutes) of email capture — the PDF-download moment is high-intent; a delayed welcome email loses most of its relevance.
- If your email platform supports it, tag these subscribers as "generator-signup" so the weekly host email (see separate template) and the January Gold Club push can be targeted specifically to this segment versus existing customers.
- Do not include a Gold Club sales pitch in this first email — per the campaign plan, the fall period is for warming the relationship, not converting. Save the harder Gold Club push for the Nov-Dec "warm + launch" phase.
