# Short-Form Social Video Concepts — FatCity Entertainment
12 low-production, phone-shootable concepts for TikTok / IG Reels / Facebook. Built for bar/restaurant host customers to film themselves, or for FatCity to film at partner venues. Grouped by format.

---

## Crowd-Reaction Format

### 1. "The Drop"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** Room mid-conversation, cards on tables, host mid-sentence — then the song hits.
- **Shot list:** Wide shot of the room right as a recognizable song starts playing; whip-pan or quick cuts to 2-3 tables reacting (pointing at cards, cheering, singing along).
- **Audio:** The actual in-room song audio — let the recognizable hook play clean, no voiceover needed.
- **On-screen text:** "The exact moment [song] plays at music bingo 🎶" (swap in the actual song each time)
- **CTA:** "Run your own night — free cards, link in bio"

### 2. "Bingo!" Reaction
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels, Facebook
- **Hook (0-2s):** Close-up on a card, marker slamming down on the last square.
- **Shot list:** Close-up on the winning card → quick cut to the winner jumping up / yelling "BINGO" → host confirming the win at the mic.
- **Audio:** Ambient room sound + a quick celebratory sound effect on the cut.
- **On-screen text:** "When you're one song away and it hits 😱"
- **CTA:** "Comment your winning song and we'll add it to a round"

### 3. Empty Room vs. Packed Room
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels, Facebook
- **Hook (0-2s):** Static shot of the empty venue, a clock or "6:45 PM" text overlay.
- **Shot list:** Same camera angle, jump cut to the same room packed and mid-round at 8:00 PM. Classic before/after transformation format.
- **Audio:** Trending "transformation" audio or a simple beat drop timed to the cut.
- **On-screen text:** "6:45 PM vs 8:00 PM on Music Bingo Thursday"
- **CTA:** "Come find out why — every Thursday, [venue name]"

## Host-Tip Format

### 4. "3 Mistakes Killing Your Music Bingo Night"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** Host talking directly to camera: "If your music bingo night isn't packed yet, it's probably one of these three things."
- **Shot list:** Talking-head host, quick B-roll cutaways illustrating each mistake (dead air between songs, a card with no theme, an empty promo board).
- **Audio:** Voiceover/direct-to-camera, no music (so the advice is clearly audible).
- **On-screen text:** Numbered captions for each mistake as the host says it (1. Overcomplicated rules / 2. Dead air / 3. No promotion runway)
- **CTA:** "Full hosting guide — link in bio"

### 5. "How Long Should a Round Actually Run?"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** "Everyone asks me this — here's the exact timing that works."
- **Shot list:** Host to camera with a phone timer visible, quick cuts showing round transitions.
- **Audio:** Direct-to-camera voiceover.
- **On-screen text:** "30-45 min per round. 2-3 rounds a night. That's it."
- **CTA:** "More host tips in our free guide — link in bio"

### 6. "What's In My Music Bingo Bag"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** Host pulling items out of a bag/box one at a time.
- **Shot list:** Overhead or POV shot revealing: printed cards, markers, backup speaker, phone with playlist ready. Classic "what's in my bag" trend format.
- **Audio:** Trending "what's in my bag" audio.
- **On-screen text:** Item labels as each is revealed.
- **CTA:** "Build your card set free — link in bio"

## Playlist-Teaser Format

### 7. "Guess the Decade"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** "Can you guess the decade from just the intro?" over a muted room shot.
- **Shot list:** Play 3-4 song intros back to back (1-2 seconds each), cut to black between each with a countdown, reveal the decade at the end.
- **Audio:** The actual song intros, sequenced.
- **On-screen text:** "3... 2... 1... which decade?" then reveal "It's an 80s round 🕺"
- **CTA:** "Run this exact round — Gold Club link in bio"

### 8. "This Week's Theme Reveal"
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels, Facebook
- **Hook (0-2s):** A card or graphic flipping over to reveal the theme.
- **Shot list:** Simple reveal animation or physical flip of a chalkboard/sign showing this week's theme (e.g., "90s Night").
- **Audio:** Short, upbeat trending sound.
- **On-screen text:** "This Thursday's theme: 90s Night 🎤"
- **CTA:** "See you at 8 — [venue name]"

### 9. "Rate These Music Bingo Songs"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** "Rating tonight's music bingo playlist out of 10."
- **Shot list:** Host or a table of guests reacting to 4-5 song snippets, holding up a number of fingers or a written score card.
- **Audio:** Real song snippets + reactions.
- **On-screen text:** Running scoreboard overlay.
- **CTA:** "What would you rate a 10? Comment below"

## Post-Event / Atmosphere Format

### 10. "Last Call Recap"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels, Facebook
- **Hook (0-2s):** Fast-cut highlight reel opening on the loudest crowd moment of the night.
- **Shot list:** 5-8 quick clips: cards being marked, a big win, the crowd singing, high-fives, host wrapping up.
- **Audio:** Upbeat trending track cut to the highlights.
- **On-screen text:** "Tonight at [venue name] 🎶"
- **CTA:** "Same time next week — tag someone who needs to come"

### 11. "POV: You're One Square Away"
- **Platform:** TikTok, IG Reels
- **Hook (0-2s):** First-person POV looking down at a nearly-complete card.
- **Shot list:** POV shot of a hand hovering over the last open square, tension-building pause, then marking it as the crowd erupts (cut to reaction).
- **Audio:** Suspenseful trending audio building to a drop, or real room audio.
- **On-screen text:** "POV: one square left and the song's about to play"
- **CTA:** "Feel this every week — link in bio"

### 12. "Behind the Bar on Bingo Night"
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels, Facebook
- **Hook (0-2s):** Bartender's POV pouring drinks with the crowd noise rising in the background.
- **Shot list:** Quick day-in-the-life style cuts: setting up the mic/speaker, printing cards, first guests arriving, room filling up.
- **Audio:** Ambient audio building into the trending track as the room fills.
- **On-screen text:** "Setting up for the busiest night of our week"
- **CTA:** "Come see what the hype is about — Thursdays at [venue name]"

## Notes for Filming
- All 12 are shootable on a phone with no additional equipment beyond a tripod or propped phone.
- Crowd-reaction clips (1, 2, 3, 10, 11) perform best captured in the moment during a live event — brief the host or a staff member to grab 10-15 seconds of footage at the loudest point of the night, every night, even if it's not used immediately. Build a footage library.
- Host-tip clips (4, 5, 6) can be filmed anytime, don't require an event, and are the easiest to batch-produce for the Sep-Oct content push.
- Always get a quick verbal OK from guests before featuring recognizable faces close-up; wide/crowd shots generally don't require this.
