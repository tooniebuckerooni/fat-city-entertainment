# The Music Bingo Handbook Is Live on Amazon

We wrote down everything we've learned running music bingo nights that pack the room, and it's finally out: [The Music Bingo Handbook](/musicbingohandbook.html) is live on Amazon in Kindle and paperback.

## What's Inside

It's a step-by-step guide, not a theory book. Six sections, in the order you'll actually use them:

- **The Setup** — the sound gear, screens, and printed cards you actually need, plus a first-night checklist.
- **Playlists That Pop** — building and sequencing game playlists on Spotify, Apple Music, or Amazon Music.
- **Hosting the Show** — openers, pacing, calling wins, and handling ties and disputes.
- **Games & Prizes** — formats beyond a straight line, and prize structures that feel generous without eating the budget.
- **Getting Booked & Paid** — how to pitch venues, what to charge, and turning one good night into a weekly residency.
- **Filling the Room** — simple promotion that actually works for bars and breweries.

## Who It's For

Bar, brewery, and restaurant owners looking for a weeknight that draws. Hosts and DJs adding music bingo to their service menu. Anyone who bought a pack of bingo cards and thought "okay… now what?"

If you're starting from zero, pair it with the [music bingo rules](/music-bingo-rules.html) and our free [bingo card generator](/bingocardgenerator.html) — that's everything you need to run a night this week.
