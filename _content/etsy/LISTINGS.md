**FAQ** (Shop Manager > Settings > Info & Appearance > FAQs)

- **What do I need to host?**
  ```
  A printer, a pen for each player and a way to play music out loud. The playlists are on Spotify and Apple Music, and the callsheet lists every song in play order if you would rather use your own music.
  ```
- **What paper size are the cards?**
  ```
  They are made for US Letter paper, printed landscape. On A4, choose "Fit to page" when you print.
  ```
- **How many people can play?**
  ```
  Each game has 250 different cards, so up to 250 players, each with a unique card.
  ```
- **Can I use these at a paid event or a bar?**
  ```
  Yes. Host as many nights as you like, at home, at work or at your venue. Please do not resell or share the files themselves.
  ```
- **Do I need a screen or software for the trivia nights?**
  ```
  No. The printable trivia nights are paper only: a host packet to read from, team answer sheets and a score sheet. The game show listings are the exception, they run in Microsoft PowerPoint on a TV or projector, and the listing says so.
  ```
