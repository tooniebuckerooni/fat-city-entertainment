Company holiday parties in Calgary and Southern Alberta

Mobile event entertainers travel to your venue anywhere in Calgary and Southern Alberta. That matters more here than in most markets: the good rooms in December are booked months out, and a supplier who travels to you means you can take the venue that is available rather than the venue that happens to have an in-house setup.

Because the gear travels too, the room does not need to be an event space. A restaurant back room, a community hall, an office floor with the desks pushed back — the audio, screens and lights arrive with the host and are set up and sound-checked hours before your guests do.

Booking timeline for a December event

Calgary's corporate December is compressed into about three weekends, and entertainment books out before venues do. Enquiring in September or October is comfortable. Enquiring at the end of November means choosing from what is left, which in practice means a Tuesday.

A quote is free, and a date is held with a deposit. If your date is firm but your details are not, book the date first — the format, the categories and the running order can all be settled later, and none of them are the thing that runs out.

What the evening looks like

Hosts arrive a few hours ahead to set up the full audio and visual rig and run a sound check. Background music plays as your guests arrive, and the cocktail hour or dinner can be DJ'd on request. If there are speeches, the PA is yours — tell us in advance and the show is built around them.

Then the hosts pick up the microphones and run the games. Expect the room to be pulled into it: bonus points for dancing, air guitars and singing along are part of how these shows work.

Choosing your format

  • Rock Jeopardy — the traditional big-screen game show, built around musical artists. Six categories and a Final Jeopardy round, with image and video clues in some categories. Christmas-themed categories are available.
  • Music Bingo — every guest gets a card and dobs off answers as the songs play. The easiest format to join late and the best choice for a large or mixed room, which is why it is consistently the most requested.
  • Fat Bottom Trivia — the pub-style trivia format, for a crowd that wants to compete properly in teams.

Christmas-themed versions are available across all three, and custom categories about your own company can be built into any of them.

Getting a quote

The quote form on this page asks for three things, and all three genuinely change the recommendation: your guest count, your date, and who is actually in the room. A staff party averaging 25 and one averaging 55 want different music, and a group of 40 wants a different format from a group of 200.

Tell us all three and we will come back with an honest recommendation, including when a smaller or cheaper option is the better fit for your night.
