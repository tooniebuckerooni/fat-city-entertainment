Why we do this

We're entertainers. We're ice-breakers.

Since 1999 we've stood in front of rooms full of strangers and watched them leave as a crowd. A good game does that. It gets people off their phones, across the table and arguing about a song lyric with someone they'd never have spoken to.

We believe that matters, especially now.

So we take the nights that work in our rooms and hand them to you, tested, printed and ready, so you can be the one who brings your people together.
